A bakery built to gather people, not just feed them.

Heartfelt Kitchen & Co. is a working bakery kitchen inside a historic home in Canton, Pennsylvania, run by Becca Horton.

Meet Becca

Self-taught, and baking for over a decade.

Becca has been baking for about 12 years, entirely self-taught. She started Heartfelt Kitchen & Co. in 2021 around five things: custom cakes, cupcakes, cookies, classes, and catering; the 5 C's.

She also teaches culinary programs at the college level, and that experience carries straight into the kitchen. It's part of why there's a full class schedule running alongside the wedding cakes and catering orders.

Selling baked goods was never really the point. Becca built Heartfelt Kitchen to be a place where people gather, celebrate, learn, and create around food.

What we believe

Food brings people together. The place that makes it should too.

Every cake, cookie box, and hands-on class comes from the same idea: food is better shared. Whether it's a wedding cake, a Saturday baking class, or a full catering spread, the goal is the same — bring people into the kitchen, not just send them home with a box.
