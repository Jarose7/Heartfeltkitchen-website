How it works

How booking works

It's simple. You reach out, Becca follows up, and you work out the details together.

01

Submit an Inquiry

Fill out the inquiry form with your event date, what it is, and roughly how many people. Becca follows up within 48 hours.

02

Design Together

Becca goes through the details with you: cake design, flavors, colors, the full spread. If you have a Pinterest board, bring it. If you have no idea, she'll show up with options.

03

Everything Made by Hand

Everything is baked from scratch in Becca's Canton kitchen. No premixes. A 50% deposit locks in your date and covers the ingredients she orders for your event.

Ready to book your event?

Drop Becca a message with your date and what you're planning. She'll follow up with what it costs and when she's available.
